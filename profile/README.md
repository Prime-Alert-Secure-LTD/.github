## About

**Prime Alert Secure Limited** develops connected security and emergency-response technology for organizations, facilities, and individuals.

Our platform sits between physical devices and operational response. It handles device communication, monitoring, alerts, incidents, customer and installer workflows, administrative operations, and the infrastructure required to keep those systems dependable in production.

The goal is straightforward: when a device reports an event, the platform should process it quickly, route it correctly, preserve a reliable record, and give the right people the information they need to respond.

## Platform architecture

Prime Alert Secure is built as a multi-surface platform connecting physical devices with cloud services and operational teams.

A typical flow looks like this:

```text
Connected device
      ↓
Device gateway and identity
      ↓
Event processing
      ↓
Alert and incident services
      ↓
Monitoring and operational interfaces
      ↓
Notification or response workflow
```

Each stage is designed around reliable delivery, traceability, controlled access, and safe recovery when networks or downstream dependencies fail.

## Engineering principles

- **Reliability**: emergency and security systems must behave predictably under real operating conditions.
- **Fast event handling**: device events and alerts should move through the platform with minimal unnecessary delay.
- **Security by design**: device identity, authentication, authorization, and data protection are part of the architecture from the start.
- **Traceability**: important events, administrative actions, and incident workflows should leave a clear operational record.
- **Resilient communication**: retries, temporary network loss, duplicate events, and device reconnects must be handled safely.
- **Operational visibility**: teams need clear insight into device state, platform health, alerts, and response activity.
- **Controlled deployment**: production changes, firmware updates, and infrastructure changes should be staged and verifiable.
